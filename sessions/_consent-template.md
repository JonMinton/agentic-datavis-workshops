## Standing rule — Chatham House by default

Views, suggestions, and mistakes are **never attributed to an individual** in the
writeup, regardless of whether that person is named below. Naming is *credit only*
(appearing in the author list or session badge); the prose says "the group", "a
participant". The rare exception — quoting someone by name — requires the explicit
per-person opt-in in the last column, and even then should be used sparingly.

| Participant | Named as author on the page? | Opt-in: named attribution of specific contributions? | How consent was given |
|---|---|---|---|
| (name) | yes / no | no (default) / yes | e.g. Zoom chat message, verbal on recording at hh:mm |

Anyone not listed here appears in the writeup only as "a participant".

Withdrawals: a participant may withdraw naming consent at any time before *or after*
publication; note the request here and remove their name from the page in the same commit.

Raw transcripts and chat exports contain speaker labels and are **never committed** to
this repo (see `.gitignore`) — they stay local and in Pocket, as accuracy sources only.
